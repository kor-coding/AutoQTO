Privacy policy

How Auto QTO PDF Viewer relates to your data. Last updated: September 2026.

This policy describes privacy practices for the Auto QTO PDF Viewer desktop application and this marketing website. It is published by Kor.

Summary

The desktop app is designed to work with PDF files you open locally. Core take-off and drawing analysis run on your machine. Sign-in is required to use the app; aside from authentication and subscription checks when you sign in or validate a paid plan, the app does not send analytics or crash telemetry, and it does not upload your PDFs for take-off processing. This website is a static marketing site hosted on GitHub Pages and does not require an account to browse.

Desktop application

Files you open

PDFs and exports you create remain under your control on your computer (and any locations you choose to save them). Drawing analysis and quantity take-off processing stay on your device. We do not receive copies of your drawings unless you voluntarily send them (for example when emailing support).

Accounts and sign-in

The desktop application requires login. When you sign in, or when the app validates your subscription, it may contact a server to authenticate your account and confirm licence status. Paid subscriptions are handled via Stripe (checkout and customer portal on the Pricing page).

Network activity

Aside from authentication and subscription checks when you sign in or validate a paid plan, the app does not make outbound network requests in normal use. PDF take-off processing does not upload your drawings to our servers. There is no marketing analytics or crash-reporting pipeline in the application.

Crash and diagnostic data

We do not collect crash telemetry or automated diagnostic reports from the desktop application.

This website

The site is static HTML/CSS/JS hosted on GitHub Pages for documentation and marketing. It does not provide user accounts. We do not use third-party analytics cookies on this site. GitHub Pages may set technical cookies or logs as part of hosting; we do not add our own analytics.

If you email siakkor.chew@glodon.com, we process the content of your message to respond. Support email correspondence is retained for up to 12 months, after which it may be deleted unless we need to keep it longer for an ongoing matter.

Third parties

We use the following processors in connection with the product and this site:

  • GitHub — hosts this website (GitHub Pages). SeeGitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Stripe — payment processor for paid subscriptions (checkout and customer portal on the Pricing page). SeeStripe’s privacy policy.

Email contact is handled via the address above; your message content is processed only to respond to your enquiry.

Children

The product is aimed at professional construction and design users. It is not directed at children.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold (typically limited to correspondence you send us, and account or billing data held by Stripe where applicable). Contact us to exercise those rights. For Stripe-held billing data, you may also use the customer portal linked from Pricing.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be noted on this page.

Contact
