FAQ

Common questions

What does a subscription unlock?

Individual paid includes Auto Bot, 3D View, and skipping the waiting-panel promo for subscriber accounts. Free use may still show a short promo, then Continue. Paid features apply when your account type supports them after Stripe checkout.

What is included in Enterprise?

Enterprise includes everything in Individual (Auto Bot, 3D View, skip promo wait) plus Save and load template for Quantity dock column headers. Packs of 10 licences per purchase; listed prices are for one pack. Contact us for multi-pack or volume arrangements.

How do I subscribe or manage billing?

Use the Stripe Pricing Table below to choose Individual or Enterprise and complete checkout. To update payment details or cancel, use Manage subscription (Stripe Customer Portal).

Do I need a subscription to use the app?

You can download and use Auto QTO without a paid plan for the core take-off workflow. Subscription unlocks Auto Bot, 3D View, skip-promo behaviour, and (on Enterprise) Save/Load Template.

What are the plan amounts?

Individual is $10/month or $100/year (USD). Enterprise is $80/month or $800/year for a pack of 10 licences. The Pricing Table shows the live Stripe prices.
