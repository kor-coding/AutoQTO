About Auto QTO PDF Viewer

Quantity take-off from PDF drawings — detect grids, rooms, and centerlines, then export.

Auto QTO PDF Viewer is a PySide6 desktop application for quantity take-off from PDF drawings. It combines a CAD-style PDF viewer with tools to confirm scale, analyse grids, detect rooms and areas, extract wall centrelines, organise quantities with spreadsheet-style formulas, and export to DXF and PDF.

Why it exists

Much of the built environment still arrives as PDF. Estimators and surveyors need more than markup — they need geometry that can be measured, edited, and handed to CAD or a bill of quantities.Auto QTO focuses on that path: detect, verify, calculate, export.

Who builds it

Built by Kor — a programming enthusiast focused on take-off solutions for people who measure drawings. The aim is practical desktop software that stays close to how quantity surveyors and estimators actually work.

Who it is for

  • Quantity surveyors preparing area and length schedules
  • Estimators building tender quantities from issued drawings
  • Architects checking room areas and coordinating overlays
  • Contractors extracting lengths and annotated sheets for site

Approach

Detection uses heuristics suited to typical architectural and structural PDFs. Results are always editable. The licence and techniques disclaimer make clear that quantities must be verified before contractual use — see the Licence page.
