AI Assist

AI Assist is a paid feature that turns a named quantity job into draft geometry on the sheet you are viewing. You do not write prompts: choose a scenario from the AI Assist menu, pick an effort level, and the app sends the open drawing to its AI specialist for that job. The reply is drawn straight into the tools you already use — area polygons, centerlines, and point markers — and listed in the Quantity Take-Off dock, ready for you to check and edit.

When to use

When you want a first-pass draft for a specific job — finish areas, steel, beam details, concrete and formwork, or tiles — on the current sheet, and you are signed in on an Individual or Enterprise plan with AI credit available.

Steps

  1. PrerequisitesSign in with a paid account, make sure you have AI credit, and open the PDF on the sheet you want measured. That page is the main input; other pages of the same file (up to 8 in total) are sent as context for schedules and sections, so there is nothing extra to upload. Only one AI Assist run can be in progress at a time.
  2. Choose a scenarioOpen the AI Assist menu on the toolbar (it only appears after a paid sign-in) and pick the job: Finish Area, Steel Structure, Beam Details, Conc & Fwk, or Tiles. Each scenario has its own fixed instructions, so you are never asked to type or paste a prompt.
  3. Pick an effort levelThe effort dialog is titled with the scenario name and says in one line what will be measured. Choose Quick (a short look at one sheet), Standard (the default takeoff), or Careful (dense or multi-page sets). The dialog shows the rate for each level. OK starts the run; Cancel does nothing and charges nothing.
  4. Wait while it runsA waiting dialog with a running clock stays up for the whole run so you can see the job is still alive. Long runs are normal — the first response can take many minutes on a dense sheet. Stop cancels the run.
  5. Read the run summaryWhen the run ends, a summary shows the effort level, elapsed time, tokens in and out, the amount charged, the rate, and your remaining credit. It also lists warnings (pending heights, assumptions, items not traced) and any drawings you should open next, such as a missing schedule or section.
  6. Check the results on the drawing and in the dockResults are drawn on the page you are viewing: areas as room polygons, linear items as centerlines (external or internal), and items without a polygon yet — such as doors, columns, or fittings — as point markers. Names from the drawing are kept in the Quantity Take-Off dock. Fix anything that is off with Edit Areas, as you would after Identify Rooms.

Tips & warnings

Note

AI Assist is a paid feature (Individual and Enterprise). Your subscription unlocks the menu; each run is paid from prepaid AI credit, bought separately as one-time packs. See Pricing for current rates.

Note

What each scenario drafts — Finish Area: finish zones as areas, plus door, window, and sanitary markers. Tiles: tiled rooms with the tile size on each record. Steel Structure: member axes as lines, with marks that cannot be lined as points. Beam Details: beam axes, section boxes where traced, and bar groups as points. Conc & Fwk: slabs and column and RC wall footprints as areas, beam and wall axes as lines, and any column or wall without a footprint as a point.

Warning

AI Assist only uses what is printed on the sheet. A missing dimension, height, finish code, or bar size stays pending — never zero — and is listed in the summary. Treat every run as a draft and verify it against the drawing before relying on tender figures.

Tip

Set or confirm scale first if you can. AI Assist will not overwrite a scale you have calibrated; it only proposes one from a printed scale when none is set yet.

Tip

If the connection drops, the app retries once after about a minute and stays open. A run that fails without returning any usage is not charged.
