Quantity Take-Off dock

The Quantity Take-Off dock (typically right; left or bottom also fine) lists detected geometry with built-in columns and groups. Click a row to gold-highlight and zoom; manage visibility and deletes from the context menu.

When to use

Throughout takeoff — after Identify Rooms or Generate Centerlines — to review, rename, hide, or delete items and to drive the view.

Steps

  1. Read the columnsBuilt-in columns include Element/Room, Length/Perimeter, Area, Volume (reserved), and Source (AUTO, EDITED, INTERNAL, EXTERNAL, and similar).
  2. GroupsItems appear under groups such as Detected Rooms and Centerlines.
  3. Click and multi-selectClick a row for gold highlight and zoom to that item. Ctrl/Shift multi-select for batch actions.
  4. Right-click actionsHide / Show / Delete. Delete asks for confirmation and cannot be undone. Hidden rows look italic/dim and their overlay hides.

Tips & warnings

Warning

Delete cannot be undone — confirm carefully. Prefer Hide if you may need the item again on this sheet.
