3D View

3D View is a paid feature for reviewing takeoff in three dimensions. You enter an expected floor height, then explore the model with zoom, pan, and rotate. Measurement results include vertical area quantity, and the Quantity Take-Off dock shows those vertical areas at the same time.

When to use

When you need wall / vertical area quantities or a spatial check of rooms and measurements — available on Individual and Enterprise paid plans.

Steps

  1. Have measurements readyIdentify rooms (and any related takeoff) on the sheet so there is geometry to lift into 3D.
  2. Open 3D ViewUse the 3D View button. A prompt asks for the expected floor height.
  3. Enter floor heightProvide the floor-to-floor (or storey) height you want applied so vertical faces can be measured.
  4. Explore the visualisationZoom, pan, and rotate the 3D view to inspect rooms and measurements.
  5. Read vertical area in the dockThe Quantity Take-Off dock shows vertical area quantities alongside the 3D results so you can schedule them with the rest of the takeoff.

Tips & warnings

Note

3D View is a paid feature (Individual and Enterprise). Floor height drives vertical area — check the unit and value before relying on figures.

Tip

Use 3D View after Edit Areas if polygons needed fixing in plan first.
