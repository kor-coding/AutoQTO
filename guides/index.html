Document & view

Opening sheets, CAD mode, pan/zoom, scale, and grid extents.

  1. 02

    Open PDF

    Open a vector PDF; page 1 loads in PDF view and unlocks Save PDF, pages, and Switch to CAD.

  2. 03

    Prev / Next Page

    Change sheet; takeoff on the previous page is cleared — recalibrate and re-identify on the new sheet.

  3. 04

    PDF view vs Switch to CAD

    PDF view for fast preview; CAD for vector extraction, tiled underlay, and live room/wall overlays.

  4. 05

    Pan and zoom

    Hand-drag to pan, wheel to zoom; click a Quantity dock row to frame that item.

  5. 06

    Ruler

    With scale set, click two points to read distance in the current unit.

  6. 07

    Calibrate Scale

    CAD only: two points on a known length, enter real distance; stored as metres.

  7. 08

    Find Grid Extents + confirm scale

    Find grid clusters, keep axis lines out of wall detection, and optionally confirm scale from grid dimensions.

  8. 19

    Reopen previously measured PDF

    Open a PDF you saved with takeoff session data, Switch to CAD to restore, then continue with Edit Areas.

Takeoff geometry

Hide clutter, identify rooms, centerlines, offsets, and edit areas.

  1. 09

    Hide Drawings

    Toolbar toggle and floating panel to hide stroke colours or picks so Identify Rooms ignores clutter.

  2. 10

    Identify Rooms

    CAD detection of enclosed rooms with door bypasses, grid/hidden ignore, and title-based names.

  3. 11

    Generate Centerlines

    From rooms, generate internal (orange) and external (blue) wall centerlines with gap limits.

  4. 12

    Rooms Offset

    Live three-room demo of GFA, all-centerline, or outer-face area modes from rooms + centerlines.

  5. 13

    Edit Areas

    Checkable edit mode with a second toolbar to select, draw, split, merge, offset, and rename.

  6. 14

    Clear Overlay

    Remove coloured overlays from the canvas; analysis lists stay in memory until page change or new detect.

Quantities & export

Quantity dock, formulas, Excel, and Save PDF.

  1. 15

    Quantity Take-Off dock

    Right dock of rooms and centerlines with highlight, multi-select, hide, and delete.

  2. 16

    User columns and formulas

    Add columns and spreadsheet-style formulas with @[], cross-row refs, Fill Down, and error codes.

  3. 17

    Export to Excel

    Export the dock tree as shown — built-in and user columns with computed values — to .xlsx.

  4. 18

    Save PDF

    Save the current page with rooms, centerlines, scale, and session data for restore and PDF XChange.
