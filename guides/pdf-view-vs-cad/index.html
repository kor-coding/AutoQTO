PDF view vs Switch to CAD

PDF view is for browsing. Switch to CAD prepares the sheet for takeoff: vectors extract in the background, a tiled underlay appears, and rooms/walls become live overlays.

When to use

Stay in PDF view to inspect a sheet quickly. Switch to CAD before Identify Rooms, Edit Areas, scale tools that need CAD, or any geometry analysis.

Steps

  1. PDF viewFast pan and zoom preview. Identify Rooms and Edit Areas are not available here.
  2. Switch to CADVectors are extracted in the background. The drawing becomes a tiled underlay; rooms and walls can appear as live overlays once you run analysis.
  3. While switchingA waiting panel can appear on CAD switch. You can still pan and zoom the drawing while tools remain greyed until the panel closes.

Tips & warnings

Tip

If the waiting panel stays open, keep navigating the sheet — see Waiting panel for free vs signed-in behaviour.
