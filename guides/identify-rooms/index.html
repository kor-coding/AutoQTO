Identify Rooms

Identify Rooms finds enclosed spaces once CAD vectors are ready. It closes door openings (red dashed bypasses while running), ignores grid axes and hidden drawings, and uses title text for names.

When to use

After Switch to CAD (and ideally after scale and Hide Drawings). Required before Generate Centerlines and useful before Edit Areas.

Steps

  1. PrerequisitesBe in CAD with vectors ready. Optionally Find Grid Extents, Calibrate Scale, and Hide Drawings first.
  2. Run Identify RoomsStart the tool. A waiting panel may appear. While running, door openings show red dashed bypasses.
  3. Review resultsEnclosed rooms appear as translucent polygons with labels. Title text on the drawing is used for names where possible. Numbers land in the Quantity Take-Off dock under Detected Rooms.
  4. What unlocksEdit Areas and Generate Centerlines become available after a successful identify.

Tips & warnings

Note

Grid axes from Find Grid Extents and colour-hidden strokes from Hide Drawings are ignored as walls.

Warning

Prev / Next Page clears rooms on that sheet — re-identify after changing page.
