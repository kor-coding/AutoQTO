Pan and zoom

Navigation is the same idea in PDF view and CAD: drag with the hand tool to pan, scroll to zoom. The Quantity dock can drive the view to a selected item.

When to use

Whenever you need to inspect linework, place scale points, or review a detected room or centerline.

Steps

  1. PanUse hand-drag to move around the sheet.
  2. ZoomUse the mouse wheel to zoom in and out on the cursor area.
  3. Frame from the dockClick a row in the Quantity Take-Off dock to highlight that item in gold and zoom the view to frame it.

Tips & warnings

Note

Pan and zoom remain available while the waiting panel is open, even when other tools are greyed.
