Hide Drawings

Hide Drawings clears ticks, dimensions, and other strokes that can pollute wall detection. Identify Rooms ignores colour-hidden strokes. Hides reset when you change page or file.

When to use

When dimension ticks, text, or hatch strokes confuse Identify Rooms — hide them before detecting rooms.

Steps

  1. Open Hide DrawingsUse the toolbar toggle to open the floating Hide Drawings panel.
  2. Hide by colourIn Color mode, click a stroke to hide all strokes of that colour.
  3. Hide random picksIn Random mode, click individual strokes or text to hide them one by one.
  4. Box-selectShift+drag a box to hide everything in the selection.
  5. Undo, show all, and EscCtrl+Z undoes the last hide batch. Show all restores visibility. Esc once stops picking; Esc again closes the panel.

Tips & warnings

Warning

Hides reset on page change or when you open a new file. Re-apply on each sheet if needed.

Tip

Identify Rooms ignores colour-hidden strokes — hide noisy layers before you detect.
