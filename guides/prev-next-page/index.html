Prev / Next Page

Multi-page PDFs are navigated with Prev / Next Page. Each sheet is its own analysis context: leaving a page clears that page’s takeoff overlays.

When to use

When the plan you need is on another page of the same PDF.

Steps

  1. Change sheetUse Prev Page or Next Page to move to the sheet you need.
  2. Takeoff is clearedRooms, centerlines, and overlays from the previous page are cleared. Recalibrate scale and re-run Identify Rooms (and later steps) on the new sheet.
  3. What is keptUser-added column names in the Quantity dock stay across page changes.

Tips & warnings

Warning

Do not assume quantities from the previous sheet still apply — analysis is per page.
