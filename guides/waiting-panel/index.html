Waiting panel

The waiting panel appears during CAD switch, Find Grid Extents, Identify Rooms, Generate Centerlines, Rooms Offset, Edit Areas commits that need rebuild, Save PDF, and Export to Excel. Tools stay grey until it closes; pan and zoom still work.

When to use

You do not open it yourself — it appears during long operations. Know the free vs signed-in behaviour so you are not blocked unexpectedly.

Steps

  1. While it is openYou can still pan and zoom the drawing. Analysis and edit tools remain greyed until the panel closes.
  2. Free usersA short promo may show, then Continue (or the panel auto-closes if the job has already run long enough).
  3. Signed inSign in to skip ads when subscribed. Payment sign-in is not in general release yet.

Tips & warnings

Note

Payment sign-in for subscriptions is not generally released yet — treat skip-ads as available when your account type supports it.
