Ruler

The Ruler is a quick spot-check once scale is calibrated or confirmed. It reports the distance between two clicks in the current unit.

When to use

To verify scale against a known dimension, or to measure an ad-hoc length without creating takeoff geometry.

Steps

  1. Ensure scale is setCalibrate Scale or accept a grid dimension scale first. The Ruler needs a valid scale.
  2. Click two pointsActivate the Ruler and click the start and end of the segment you want to measure.
  3. Read the distanceThe distance appears in the current unit (derived from the stored metre scale).

Tips & warnings

Warning

Without scale, ruler readings are not meaningful real-world lengths.
