Techniques disclaimer — verify all quantities

Important: Grid analysis, room and area detection, wall centreline extraction, and related features rely on heuristics and automated computer vision / geometry techniques. They are aids to measurement, not certified survey outputs.

  • Results may miss elements, invent false positives, or mis-scale if the PDF scale is wrong.
  • Always verify quantities against the drawings and your professional judgement before using them in tenders, contracts, valuations, or construction.
  • The authors and contributors accept no liability for loss arising from reliance on unverified automated take-off, to the extent permitted by the Apache-2.0 licence and applicable law.
